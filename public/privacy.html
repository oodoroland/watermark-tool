Questions or requests: roland@oodoroland.com

Your images

Watermarking happens on my server, not in your browser, so your images and logo are uploaded to it. This is exactly what happens to them:

  1. They arrive over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and are written to a temporary folder on the server.
  2. The server adds the watermark and sends the result back to you.
  3. The uploaded files are deleted as soon as that request finishes, whether it succeeded or failed. If the server ever stops in the middle of a request, the temporary folder is emptied the next time it starts.

While you adjust the settings, the page sends a smaller copy of your first image (at most 1000 pixels wide) for the live preview. Those copies follow the same steps and are deleted the same way.

Nobody views your images. They are not stored, backed up, shared, sold, or used to train any software or AI model.

The images you download have their metadata removed. That includes camera details and, if your photo had it, GPS location.

Other information the server handles

WhatWhyHow long
Your IP address, held in the server's memory To limit how many requests one visitor can make, so the free service stays usable for everyone Up to 15 minutes, never written to disk
Web server access logs: IP address, time, page or API address requested, browser type To keep the service running, investigate abuse, and fix problems Up to 14 days, then deleted automatically
Error logs: the technical error message when processing fails To fix bugs Up to 14 days. These contain no images.

The settings you choose (watermark text, colour, position and so on) are sent with each request and are not kept after it.

Legal basis

I process your images only to provide the service you asked for: you upload them to get them back watermarked. I keep the short-term logs and rate-limit data on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping a free public service secure and available.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and has no advertising or tracking pixels. I use Umami for basic visit statistics, so I can see how many people use the tools and where they came from.

Umami receives the page you visited, the site that sent you, your browser and device type, your language, and your approximate location. It processes your IP address in passing to work out a visitor identifier and that location, and according to its privacy policy does not store the IP address in analytics records. Umami never receives your images or your watermark text. If you block its script in your browser, the tool works exactly the same.

Who else is involved

The server is rented from a hosting provider, which runs the hardware it sits on, and your images exist on it only for the seconds described above. Umami provides the visit statistics described above. No other company receives your data from me.

If you are outside Nigeria, your images are processed on a server that may be in another country. They are not kept there.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. In practice the only data I can tie to you is the access log entry, for up to 14 days. To find it I would need your IP address and the approximate time of your visit. Email roland@oodoroland.com and I will reply within 5 business days.

If you are not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or to the data protection authority where you live.
